NATION OF TEMPEST

💰 Economy

Welcome to the economy of Tempest. Earn, trade and spend your wealth throughout the nation.

💠 Magicules

Magicules are the main currency of the Nation of Tempest. Use them to participate in the server economy and trade with other citizens.

🪙 Owo

Owo is another currency used by the community. Magicules and Owo can also be exchanged according to the server's current exchange system.

🔄 Exchange Rate

1 Magicule = 1 Owo

💸 Trading

Citizens can trade their currencies with each other. Always double-check trades before confirming them.

🏆 How to Earn

  • 💬 Stay active in the server
  • 🎉 Participate in server activities
  • 🔄 Trade with other members
  • 🎁 Participate in server events and rewards

⚠️ Economy Rules

Exploiting bugs, using alternate accounts to abuse rewards, scamming, or manipulating the economy is not allowed.
