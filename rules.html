NATION OF TEMPEST

👑 Laws of the Demon Lord

By order of the Demon Lord, these laws govern the Nation of Tempest.

Breaking them may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans depending on the severity.

⚔️ I. Respect the Nation

Treat every citizen with respect. No harassment, bullying, discrimination, or unnecessary hostility.

💬 II. Keep the Chat Civil

No excessive spam, message flooding, annoying repeated pings, or deliberately disrupting conversations.

🚫 III. No NSFW or Inappropriate Content

Keep Tempest safe and comfortable for everyone. No sexual, pornographic, or excessively graphic content.

🛡️ IV. No Raiding or Malicious Behavior

No raids, server nuking, malicious links, scam attempts, or attempts to disrupt Tempest.

📢 V. No Unapproved Advertising

Don't advertise other servers, social accounts, or services without staff permission.

🤖 VI. Use Bots Properly

Use bot commands in their designated channels. Don't abuse commands or intentionally spam bots.

💠 VII. No Economy Abuse

No exploiting bugs, farming through alts, scamming, or manipulating the Magicule/owo systems.

🎭 VIII. Don't Impersonate Others

Don't impersonate the Demon Lord, staff, bots, or other members.

🧠 IX. Follow Staff Decisions

Staff are here to maintain order. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it respectfully rather than starting drama.

🎫 X. Keep Tickets Open When Needed

If you create a ticket, don't immediately close it or abandon it. Give staff time to respond and keep the ticket open until your issue is resolved.

📜 XI. Discord's Rules Apply

All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines in addition to Tempest's laws.

👑 XII. The Demon Lord's Final Decree

Staff may take action against behavior that seriously harms the community even if it isn't specifically listed above.
